# Statement lock: the actual boundary determinant is purely imaginary

Use the literal ordered roots R,z,z-inverse,O. Construct a permutation by
reversing the stable and exterior lists and swapping the two oscillatory
positions. Its sign is -1 because the two identical reversal signs cancel.
Prove this permutation sends every actual characteristic root to its
complex conjugate, for m >= 2 and every real theta.

Apply the explicit permutation to the columns of the actual boundary matrix
to prove conjugate(D_n(theta)) = -D_n(theta), hence Re D_n(theta)=0.
No eigenvalue assumption, nonzero determinant premise or normalized-error
reality hypothesis is allowed. Normalizer reality remains separate.
