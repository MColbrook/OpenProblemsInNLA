# Statement lock: nonvanishing through the upper endpoint

For m >= 2 and 0 < theta <= pi, an equality between two entries of the
actual characteristic-root list implies equal indices or the two
oscillatory positions m-1 and m. This records the only possible collision
at pi; it does not claim full distinctness there.

Consequently an injective sublist containing at most one of those two
positions remains injective for every 0 < theta <= pi. Its normalized
Vandermonde is nonzero on the whole closed interval [0,pi], using distinct
tangents at zero. If a subset of cardinality m separates the two
oscillatory positions, both its sublist and its complement have this
property, so its actual normalized boundary coefficient never vanishes
on [0,pi]. This is the denominator nonvanishing needed after (18).
