# Statement lock: real factors in the determinant normalizer

Define stableRootList m theta ell to be the actual r_(ell+1), indexed by
Fin(m-1), and exteriorRootList to be its pointwise reciprocal. Prove
conjugation reverses each list. Identify Q exactly with the product of
the exterior list, including multiplicities and indexing.

Prove Q is real and nonzero, Q(0)=1, and its real part is positive for
theta >= 0 and m >= 2, using continuity and the intermediate value theorem.

Prove conjugation of each actual Vandermonde determinant introduces the
same reversal sign. Therefore the product V(R)V(O) is real, without
assuming either factor separately real. These results provide the real
factors in the normalizer (16); the complete normalization remains a
separate identity.
