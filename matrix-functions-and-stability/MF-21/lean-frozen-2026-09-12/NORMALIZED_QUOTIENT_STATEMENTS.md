# Statement lock: bounded normalized coefficient quotients

For subsets S,T of the characteristic-root positions, both of cardinality
m, define the quotient of the two constructed normalized boundary
coefficients. For theta != 0 this equals the quotient of the original
Laplace coefficients, by cancellation of their proved common power.

If T separates the two oscillatory positions and m >= 2, prove the quotient
is smooth at every point of [0,pi], including zero and pi. Derive an actual
positive constant bounding its complex norm and the norm of its real
derivative throughout that interval. Neither boundedness nor a removable
singularity is assumed. This is a coefficient-ratio ingredient for (18),
not yet a proof of the full normalized determinant estimate.
