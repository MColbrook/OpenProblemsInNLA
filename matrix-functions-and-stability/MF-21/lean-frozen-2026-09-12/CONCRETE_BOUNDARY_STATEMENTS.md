# Actual boundary determinant: statement lock

Define D_n(θ) to be the actual equation (13) matrix determinant with the
ordered `characteristicRoots m θ`, not an abstract matrix or assumed root
list. For m≥2 and 0<θ<π prove

```
(∃ j, 1≤j ∧ j≤n ∧ eigenvalue m n j = symbol m θ) ↔ D_n(θ)=0.
```

Also specialize the proved boundary-kernel/eigenspace dimension equality to
this actual root list. All root hypotheses must be discharged by the
construction and its tested properties.

Each root coordinate is smooth on the real line. At θ=π the two
oscillatory roots coincide, hence D_n(π)=0 by equality of two columns.
That endpoint is explicitly excluded from the eigenvalue criterion.

These are the exact, unnormalized statements in manuscript Lemma 3.
The nonvanishing normalizer, phase formula and error bounds remain to prove.
