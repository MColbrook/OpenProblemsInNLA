# Statement lock: actual normalized error weights

Let S+ be the already defined exterior-root set plus the z position. Prove
it has cardinality m and separates the two oscillatory positions. Thus its
constructed normalized boundary coefficient is nonzero on [0,pi].

Define the phase multiplier U(theta) = exp(i*((m-1)*theta+2*psi(theta))),
using the existing sum of individual phases, and define

  A_S(theta) = normalizedBoundaryRatio(S,S+)(theta) / (2*i*U(theta)).

This is the expression for the manuscript coefficient a_S after dividing
its numerator and denominator by the actual leading Laplace coefficient.
The equality with (18)'s displayed V(R)V(O) denominator is a separate
leading-coefficient algebra obligation; it is not assumed or claimed here.

Prove A_S is smooth at every point of [0,pi] and derive a single positive
constant (depending on m only) that bounds both |A_S| and |A'_S| there for
every subset S of cardinality m. No coefficient bound is a hypothesis.
The finiteness argument is symbolic; no enumeration or numerical tests.
