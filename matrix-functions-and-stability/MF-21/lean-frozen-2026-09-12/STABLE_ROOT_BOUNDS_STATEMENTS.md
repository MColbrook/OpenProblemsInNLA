# Uniform root bounds: statement lock

For the exact manuscript parameters `κ_ell`, `1 ≤ ell < m`, with `m ≥ 2`,
prove a single `c > 0`, independent of ell and θ, such that the actual
root satisfies `norm(r_ell(θ)) ≤ exp(-c*θ)` for `0 ≤ θ ≤ π`.
This strengthens the already proved individual-parameter existence by a
finite minimum; no estimate is assumed.

For any `κ.re > 0`, also prove that the actual logarithmic derivative
`deriv (stableRootCurve κ) θ / stableRootCurve κ θ` is bounded in norm
on `[0,π]`. This follows from proved smoothness, nonvanishing, and
compactness; it is the last bound asserted in manuscript Lemma 2.

These statements do not yet bound the normalized determinant coefficients
or their derivatives. No numerical interval computation is required.
