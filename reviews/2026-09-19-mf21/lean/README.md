# Historical development work area

The complete original development sources and logs are retained in
[`../lean-development-source.tar.gz`](../lean-development-source.tar.gz).
This archive preserves the final development paths and source bytes. Earlier
receipts record the hashes of their then-current source snapshots.
The local working copies are ignored to avoid publishing a duplicate proof tree.

The maintained, independently specified project is
[`../../../matrix-functions-and-stability/MF-21/lean`](../../../matrix-functions-and-stability/MF-21/lean).
Its `reviews/packaging-map.json` maps development files and hashes to canonical
files. Canonicalization only renamed imports, separated trusted definitions
from Challenge, and refreshed status comments.
